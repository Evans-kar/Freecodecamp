Jubilee Year of Christ Event

You are all welcomed to join us as we commemorate the end of the Jubilee Year of Chirst.

When: 31st December Where:Our Lady of Fatima Catholic Church

Order of Events

Introductory Rites
The opening part of the Mass that prepares the congregation to worship.
Entrance Procession and Opening Hymn
The priest and ministers enter as the congregation sings.
Sign of the Cross
The Mass begins in the name of the Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.
Greeting
The priest greets the people, inviting them into prayer.
Penitential Act
The congregation acknowledges sins and asks for God’s mercy.
Kyrie (Lord, Have Mercy)
A prayer asking for compassion and forgiveness.
Gloria
A hymn of praise to God, usually sung on Sundays and feast days.
Opening Prayer (Collect)
The priest gathers the prayers of the people and offers them to God.
Liturgy of the Word
Scripture readings and reflection on God’s Word.
First Reading
Usually taken from the Old Testament.
Responsorial Psalm
A sung or spoken response to the first reading.
Second Reading
A reading from the New Testament letters.
Gospel Acclamation
An Alleluia sung before the Gospel reading.
Gospel Reading
A reading from one of the four Gospels.
Homily
The priest explains and reflects on the Scripture readings.
Profession of Faith (Creed)
The congregation professes its beliefs.
Prayers of the Faithful
Intercessory prayers for the Church and the world.
Liturgy of the Eucharist
The central part of the Mass where the Eucharist is consecrated.
Preparation of the Gifts
Bread and wine are brought to the altar.
Eucharistic Prayer
The priest offers the central prayer of thanksgiving and consecration.
The Lord’s Prayer
The congregation prays the “Our Father.”
Sign of Peace
Worshippers share a sign of peace with one another.
Breaking of the Bread (Lamb of God)
The host is broken as a sign of unity.
Communion
The faithful receive the Body and Blood of Christ.
Prayer After Communion
A closing prayer of thanksgiving.
Concluding Rites
The Mass concludes with a blessing and dismissal.
Final Blessing
The priest blesses the congregation.
Dismissal
The people are sent forth to live out their faith.
Recessional Hymn
A closing song as the ministers leave.

After Mass events

  • Greeting and socializing with fellow parishioners
  • Meeting the priest or deacon after Mass
  • Announcements or ministry sign-ups in the church lobby
  • Fellowship gatherings (coffee, tea, or light refreshments)
  • Religious education or youth group meetings
  • Prayer or quiet reflection inside the church
  • Volunteering for parish activities or outreach programs
  • Attending Bible study or faith formation classes
  • Community service or charitable outreach events
  • Spending time with family and friends
