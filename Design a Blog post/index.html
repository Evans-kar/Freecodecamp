The Power of Habits

The Power of Habit by Charles Duhigg explores how habits work, why they exist, and how they can be changed. The book explains that habits operate in a three-step loop: cue, routine, and reward. By understanding this loop, individuals and organizations can identify what triggers their habits and intentionally reshape them for better outcomes. Duhigg uses real-life stories from neuroscience, business, and everyday life to show how habits influence personal success, productivity, and decision-making. The central message is that while habits may feel automatic, they are not destiny—once we understand how they function, we gain the power to change them and improve our lives.
